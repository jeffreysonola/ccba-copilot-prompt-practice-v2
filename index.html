The art and science of prompting

Give Copilot the ingredients for a useful response

You do not need a perfect prompt. Start with the outcome you need, then add the context, sources, and expectations that will help Copilot produce a response you can act on.

Goal

What do you want Copilot to do?

State the task or decision clearly: summarize, compare, analyze, draft, or recommend.

Context

Why do you need it, and who is involved?

Add the audience, business situation, priorities, constraints, and decision being prepared.

Source

Which information should Copilot use?

Point to the relevant document, workbook, email thread, meeting, work data, or web sources.

Expectations

How should Copilot respond?

Define the format, level of detail, tone, evidence, structure, and action you expect.

Remember: Prompting is a conversation. Review the first response, challenge assumptions, add missing context, and ask Copilot to improve the result.
